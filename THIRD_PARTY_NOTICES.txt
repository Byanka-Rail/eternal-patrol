ETERNAL PATROL voicepack-v3 mirrors the official FP32 ONNX model assets without
weight conversion, quantization, training, or fine-tuning. Runtime distribution
is hosted by the Byanka-Rail/eternal-patrol GitHub Release. The model LICENSE is
included inside the voice pack. AI-generated crew speech is disclosed in the UI.

Reason for v3:
The previous v2 FP16 pack could load on desktop validation but Android
CPUExecutionProvider failed at runtime on FP16 operators such as
com.microsoft.Gelu. v3 keeps official FP32 computation for CPU compatibility.
